How it works

Machine-scans public tenders

Every day at 06:00 CET our scanner queries the official EU TED API and UK Contracts Finder — the same databases procurement teams use. No web scraping, no guesswork.

Filters for your market

BESS, solar, EV charging, grid/substation, heat pumps, energy management. Filtered by country, value threshold and deadline so you only see what fits your business.

Delivers a ready-to-act brief

Each opportunity arrives as a one-page brief: buyer, contact, estimated value, deadline, scope, and a suggested next action. Business tier includes a pre-drafted proposal outline.
